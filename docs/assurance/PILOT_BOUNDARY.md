# Controlled Pilot Boundary

A controlled pilot may use an approved, reversible decision domain, synthetic
or explicitly approved limited data, named policy and decision owners,
institution-managed identity, private storage, observed Postgres/Redis, and a
human assistance route. It should begin in shadow mode and compare explanation
and routing behaviour with institutional practice.

A controlled pilot must not:

- make or communicate an adverse outcome as an automated institutional decision;
- write to PeopleSoft, a student record, email, or another external workflow;
- automatically turn a handbook or OCR output into policy;
- use unapproved external AI processing on institutional material;
- use a shared administrator account or a browser-supplied role/domain claim;
- treat unresolved accessibility, retention, identity, or incident gates as
  product acceptance.

The intended product role is a **transparency layer**: it shows the current
institutional state, the applicable published rule, the evidence/facts relied
upon, and the condition that requires human consideration. It does not tell a
student what academic choice to make or replace an authorised committee.
